I am a 5th year Ph.D. Student at the Center for Research in Computer Vision(CRCV), University of Central Florida, advised by <a href='https://www.crcv.ucf.edu/person/mubarak-shah/'>Dr. Mubarak Shah</a>. My primary research focuses on Geolocalization, Multimodal Learning Vision Language Models and Multi-agent Frameworks. I have also worked in Federated Learning, Privacy Preserving Computer Vision and Signal Processing.  

I graduated with Bachelors in Technology(B. Tech) in Computer Science and Engineering(CSE) from <a href='https://www.iitp.ac.in/'>Indian Institute of Technology Patna(IITP)</a> in 2020.
